Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new child?

Tap the "+" button on the home screen and fill in the required information about your child.

How do I record growth data?

Select your child from the Growth tab, tap "Add Record", and enter the height and weight measurements.

How do I add health records?

Go to the Health tab, select your child, and choose the type of record you want to add (vaccination, disease, allergy, or checkup).

How do I export my data?

Go to Settings, tap "Export Data", and choose your preferred export format.

How do I backup my data?

Go to Settings, tap "Backup Data", and follow the instructions to create a backup file.
