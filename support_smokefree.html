SmokeFree Technical Support

Welcome to the SmokeFree quit smoking assistant app. This page provides frequently asked questions and technical support information to help you better use our application.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is my data stored?
SmokeFree stores all data locally on your device. We do not upload your personal data to any servers, ensuring your privacy and security. Data is stored using your device's secure storage mechanisms to ensure information is not lost.
How do I reset my quit smoking progress?
You can reset your quit smoking progress by clicking the settings icon in the top right corner of the app, then selecting the "Clear All Data" option. Please note that this action is irreversible and will delete all saved quit smoking records and statistical data.
App notifications are not reminding me on time
Please ensure you have allowed SmokeFree to send notifications in your device settings. For iOS devices, go to "Settings > Notifications > SmokeFree" and make sure notifications are enabled. Also, check that the reminder settings within the app are correctly configured. If the problem persists, try reinstalling the app.
How do I view my quit smoking history?
On the app's main page, click the "Calendar" button to view your quit smoking check-in history. The calendar view will display all your check-in dates, helping you visually understand your persistence.
How much storage space does the app use?
The SmokeFree app itself is very lightweight, typically using less than 30MB of storage space. Even with long-term use, it generates very little data and will not burden your device's storage.
Does the app support data backup?
The current version of SmokeFree does not support cloud backup. We recommend regularly backing up your entire device data to prevent data loss. In future versions, we plan to add data export and backup features.

Technical Troubleshooting

App crashes or becomes unresponsive
If the app crashes or becomes unresponsive, please try the following steps:
  1. Close the app and restart it
  2. Restart your device
  3. Ensure your device's operating system is up to date
  4. Uninstall and reinstall the app
If the problem persists, please contact our technical support team.
App cannot be installed or updated
Please ensure your device has sufficient storage space and a stable network connection. For iOS devices, make sure you are using iOS 12.0 or higher. If the problem persists, try restarting your device or temporarily turning off VPN services.
Statistical data displays incorrectly
If you find that your smoke-free days or other statistical data is displaying incorrectly, it may be due to timezone settings or date synchronization issues. Please ensure your device's date and time settings are correct. If the problem persists, you can try clearing the app data and starting over (please note that this will delete all historical records).

System Requirements

  • iOS 12.0 or higher
  • Android 8.0 or higher
