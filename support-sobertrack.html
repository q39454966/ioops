Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start tracking my sobriety?

Simply download the app, tap the "Record" button to begin your journey. You can set your start date and track your progress from there.

How do I set up daily reminders?

Go to Settings > Notifications to customize your reminder preferences. You can set the time and frequency of your daily check-ins.

Can I export my progress data?

Yes, you can export your data by going to Settings > Data Export. Your data will be exported in a readable format.

Common Issues

App Notifications Not Working

Please check your device settings to ensure notifications are enabled for SoberTrack. Go to your device Settings > Notifications > SoberTrack.

Data Not Syncing

Ensure you have a stable internet connection. If the issue persists, try logging out and logging back in.
